1. Introduction

Welcome to AgroVeda (referred to as "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of Indian farmers and users who access our mobile application and related agricultural services.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, transfer, and protect your personal and agricultural data when you install and use the AgroVeda mobile application.

Our Commitment to Farmers: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or farm records to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing firms. Data collected is used solely to provide and improve tailored agricultural advisory services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide relevant agricultural assistance, weather warnings, market rates, and community features, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Phone Number: Used for secure authentication via one-time password (OTP) through Firebase Phone Authentication.
  • Farmer Name & Location: Name, village, taluka/tehsil, district, and state to personalize your experience and localize mandi market prices.
  • Farm Profile: Total land size in acres or vigha, primary irrigation type (drip, sprinkler, flood, canal, borewell, rain-fed), and soil characteristics.

B. Crop Records & Farm Data

  • Crops Cultivated: Crop name, variety, sowing date, growth stage, acreage, and farm notebook entries.
  • Crop Health Images: Photographs of leaves, stems, or fruits submitted for AI-powered disease diagnosis and pest identification.

C. Voice & Audio Data

  • Audio Inquiries: When you use voice search or the voice-enabled AI farming assistant, audio input is captured temporarily to transcribe speech into text queries. Audio recordings are not permanently stored, sold, or used for voice profiling.

D. Location Data

  • Precise & Coarse GPS Coordinates: Collected only with your explicit permission when fetching live weather forecasts, precipitation probabilities, localized agricultural advisories, and nearby farm machinery/service providers. Location data is never used to track you in the background.

E. Technical & Diagnostic Information

  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, crash logs, and performance metrics collected automatically to ensure app stability.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

AgroVeda requests standard Android/iOS permissions strictly necessary for its features:

Permission Technical Key Purpose / Why Needed
Internet Access INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to synchronize crop data, retrieve APMC mandi prices, fetch weather forecasts, and access advisory content.
Approximate & Precise Location ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Used to deliver hyper-local weather predictions (rain, temperature, humidity) and show nearby APMC mandi commodity prices.
Camera & Gallery CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Allows you to take or upload pictures of diseased crops for instant AI detection, as well as upload profile and community pictures.
Audio Recording RECORD_AUDIO Enables hands-free voice input to ask farming queries in regional languages (Gujarati, Hindi, English).
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sends critical crop spray reminders, severe weather alerts, government scheme deadlines, and community replies.
Boot Completed RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores scheduled reminders (such as crop irrigation or livestock vaccination alerts) when the device restarts.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Farming Assistance: Delivering personalized crop stage recommendations, fertilizer calculations, and spray dosage guides.
  • Disease Diagnosis: Analyzing uploaded crop photographs using computer vision models to identify crop pests and fungal/bacterial diseases.
  • Weather & Disaster Alerts: Providing timely frost, heatwave, rainfall, and storm warnings to prevent harvest losses.
  • Community & Market Connectivity: Facilitating peer farmer discussions, community knowledge sharing, and connecting farmers with local agricultural machinery/service providers.
  • App Quality & Security: Detecting software crashes, fixing bugs, preventing spam, and securing farmer accounts.

6. Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement multi-layered administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your mobile device and backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud records are housed on Google Cloud Platform's ISO 27001, SOC 2, and SOC 3 compliant data centers.
  • Local On-Device Storage: Personal farm notes, offline crops, and calculator records are saved locally using an encrypted SQLite database (Drift).

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy and industry privacy standards, you have complete control over your data:

  • Data Retention: We retain account data as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with services.

8. Children's Privacy

AgroVeda is an agricultural assistance application designed for adult farmers and agricultural professionals. The application is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or 18 years in India), and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for deletion.

9. Agricultural Advisory Disclaimer

While AgroVeda strives to provide accurate agronomic recommendations, weather forecasts, disease detection, and APMC mandi rates:

  • AI disease identification models provide indicative suggestions and should not replace on-ground examination by certified agronomists or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) experts.
  • Weather forecasts are inherently probabilistic and subject to sudden regional atmospheric shifts.
  • APMC mandi rates are sourced from market feeds and may fluctuate during physical trading sessions.
