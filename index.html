About AgroVeda

AgroVeda is an all-in-one smart agriculture application developed to empower Indian farmers with data-driven farming decisions:

  • AI Crop Doctor: Upload photos of affected crops to identify diseases, nutrient deficiencies, and pests with organic and chemical remedies.
  • Hyper-Local Weather Forecasts: Hourly and multi-day rain predictions, humidity, wind speeds, and agricultural weather advisories.
  • APMC Mandi Rates: Daily market price updates across key agricultural commodities and regional mandi yards.
  • Farm Calculator: Precise spray dose formulas, fertilizer bag estimators, and land area unit conversions (Vigha / Acre / Hectare).
  • Farm Machinery & Labour Booking: Directory to find local tractor operators, harvesting machinery, sprayers, and farm labour.
  • Community & Farmer Network: Discussion board where farmers can exchange experiences, techniques, and ask fellow growers for advice.

Data Privacy & Safety Highlights

Farmer-Centric Principles:
  • We never sell or rent your personal contact or agricultural details.
